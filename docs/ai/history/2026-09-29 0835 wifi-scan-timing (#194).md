# How long a Wi-Fi scan takes to reach the list (#194)

**Prompts**, on PR #218: "as written, how long does scanning take to update
the list", then "check networkmanager's timing on this device", then — offered
three fixes — "Yes do all three".

## As it was

Neither UI waited for a scan. The button asked for one (`wifi_scan` returns in
under 10 ms) and said "Scanning…"; the results arrived on the next ordinary
poll, every 6 s. Neither UI scanned when its screen opened, though
`ManagementService::wifi_scan`'s doc says a UI should. "Scanning…" never
cleared.

## Measured

On the dev box's Realtek dongle (`wlx28187845b61d`, connected, carrying the SSH
session), NetworkManager 1.54.3, `RequestScan` then polling `LastScan` every
50 ms, five runs 20 s apart:

| run | `RequestScan` returned | `LastScan` moved | APs known after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6 ms | 3.39 s | 16 |
| 2 | 9 ms | 3.12 s | 20 |
| 3 | 10 ms | 3.34 s | 23 |
| 4 | 9 ms | 3.00 s | 24 |
| 5 | 8 ms | 3.93 s | 27 |

* **A scan is 3–4 s**, so a list update took 3–10 s: the scan, plus up to one
  6 s poll.
* **One scan misses networks.** The count grew every run, because
  NetworkManager keeps what earlier scans saw.
* **NetworkManager rarely scans on its own while connected.** Its last scan
  was ~4 minutes old before the runs.
* **Back-to-back scans are not refused.** Two `RequestScan`s with no gap both
  succeeded — which disproves the reason `LinuxWifiReader::scan` gave for
  swallowing every refusal ("usually a scan is already running").
* **Over SSH, a scan is refused by polkit**:
  `org.freedesktop.NetworkManager.PermissionDenied: … wifi.scan request
  failed: not authorized`. `wifi.scan` is granted to an active local session;
  the runs above were as root. On a kiosk `lunchboxd` is in that session, so
  this is the dev/SSH case — but swallowed, it showed "Scanning…" forever.

## What changed

1. A `PermissionDenied` scan is `WifiError::NotAuthorized` (403). Other
   refusals (radio off) are still only logged. `NotAuthorized`'s sentence
   became "not allowed to manage Wi-Fi", true of a scan as of a write.
2. Both UIs scan when the Wi-Fi view opens — only once a read has shown a
   radio, or a device without one would report an error on every open.
3. After any scan request both refresh every 2 s until a scan newer than the
   request shows up, or 15 s pass. The web clears "Scanning…" when it does.

"Newer than the request" is worked out from `last_scan_age_s`, which is an age
at the moment the device answered, since the device's clock means nothing to
the phone or the browser:

* **Companion:** a read counts only if `readStartedAt - age ≥ requestedAt`,
  with `readStartedAt` taken before the RPC. A read already in flight when the
  scan was requested can never pass.
* **Web:** react-query exposes when a list *arrived* (`dataUpdatedAt`), not
  when its fetch began, and the check also runs against the list cached at the
  moment of the request. `dataUpdatedAt - age ≥ requestedAt` rejects the
  cached list, but a fetch in flight across the request can still pass if
  NetworkManager scanned in the second before it. The cost is only ending the
  fast refresh early.

## Not verified

Neither UI was run against a device for this; the phone was disconnected and
the dev box's adapter cannot be paired by `pair.sh` (see
`2026-09-28 2156 wep-support (#194).md`). The web UI has tests for the scan on
open, for not scanning without a radio, and for "Scanning…" clearing within
the fast refresh.
